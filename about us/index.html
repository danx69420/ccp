About Car Club of Pune

Car Club of Pune is a community of car lovers, collectors, and enthusiasts from across the city. Our goal is simple — to bring people together through a shared love for automobiles, as we celebrate Pune’s motoring spirit and the cars that call the city their home. Pune has always possessed a rich automotive lineage, and we strive to be the modern torchbearers of that legacy.

We welcome everyone, from seasoned collectors to young enthusiasts and families who enjoy cars. Through meet-ups, drives, and curated events, we create opportunities for people to connect, share stories, and build lasting memories. Whether it's an early morning drive navigating the sweeping roads of the Sahyadris or an evening showcase under the city lights, these moments forge bonds that go far beyond the tarmac.

Our community celebrates everything from timeless classics — including 90-year-old Rolls-Royces — to modern performance cars like Ferraris and Lamborghinis. Each vehicle has a unique story, and each owner plays a vital role in keeping that story alive. We take pride in gathering a diverse fleet that represents the very best of automotive engineering, design, and history.

Car Club of Pune is not just about cars; it’s about the people behind them. We aim to preserve automotive heritage while inspiring the next generation to appreciate and enjoy the world of cars. By bringing these magnificent machines out of the garage and into the public eye, we ensure that the roar of the engines and the elegance of their design continue to captivate hearts for decades to come.
